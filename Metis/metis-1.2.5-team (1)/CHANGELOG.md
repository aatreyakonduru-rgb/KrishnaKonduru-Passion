# Mētis changelog

## Unreleased
- Asker-aware answers and a stricter source check (review items: who is asking, layers, hallucination). (1) New `tools/audience.py`: the KB is read as four layers (meaning, rules, design, code; from a page's front matter `layer:`, else its folder, else the sources it cites) and five audiences (business, developer, tech_lead, architect, support) each weight those layers differently. `answers.compose/ask` take `audience` and `context_pack.build` / `--audience` / MCP `metis_context_pack` take it too; the UI Ask and Pack forms have a selector; the profile's `audience.people` and `audience.default` choose one when none is picked. The lens orders matching lines and pages by layer weight and sets the number of link lines in an answer and the impact share of a pack. It never adds, hides or rewrites a line, never touches a tag or a clearance, and the answer says what it did, what did not fit and, when every line comes from one layer, that the order is the same for every audience. Pack ids include the audience. (2) `verify_kb.py`: with `verify.strict_tags` a repo line-range tag now needs a quote (before, only `file:` and `kb:` tags did, so a real range with an unrelated claim passed). New `verify.support_min` (0 = off, answers always use at least 0.2): a fact line must share that part of its content words with the quotes it cites; code names are split (`VARCHAR_IGNORECASE`) and a few code words map to plain words. It is a lexical screen, not a judge, and it skips `Inferred:` lines and lines under 4 content words. New tests `test_audience.py` and `test_verify_support.py`. Limits: the audience weights are starting values and have not been tested on real questions or with real readers; a KB with pages at one layer only gives every audience the same lines; summaries and the layer tree are still not part of answering, and Ask still scores lines with its own matcher rather than the hybrid search.

- Content search, hybrid retrieval, unanswered report and chat. New `tools/kb_search.py` indexes heading chunks into `<KB>/.metis/search.sqlite` with BM25 (FTS5 when available, pure-Python fallback), 3x title/heading boost, glossary synonym expansion (weight 0.6 in Python engine), and facets for folder, type, owner, open questions and stale reviews. - Metis 2.0 UI, Ask. The confidence score is computed from the KB lines found, never claimed by a model: 100 means one line states the answer in the question's own words; joined lines cap at 92, `Inferred:` lines at 80, a part not in the KB or an open question at 84; 0 means nothing found. Bands: High from 85, Medium from 60, Low from 1. When an answer has no checked lines or is below High, a guidance card shows the searched words, known and unknown words, typo options, nearest pages and a clarify box. A Devin session can ask the person at most 2 questions per round and 2 rounds (`tasks.py ask-user`, `wait`, `reply`, status `waiting-user`); a reply says where to look and is never a source. Like and dislike are thumb buttons; a dislike opens the reason form with no JavaScript. Skills `metis-ask` and `metis-tasks` updated. The `Inferred:` rule for combined lines is a skill instruction and is not enforced by the checker.

- Trust signals. New `tools/trust.py` (`page`, `edge`): raw facts per link and per page, no score. A link shows its origin (parsed, catalogue, ai-proposed), whether its source tag resolves and its quote is found (`verified`, `resolves, no quote`, `does not resolve: <why>`) and whether the cited repo file changed since the build (`changed since build`, `cannot compare`, `unchanged since build`). A page shows tag and source-problem counts, changed cited sources, last KB commit, who certified it and whether it changed since, and the open questions blocking it. The context pack puts these on every impact line and in a Signals column (`--no-signals` leaves them out). `build_plan.py` gets `changed_since_build`. A verified quote means the words are in the source, not that the statement is right.
- Review queue and one PR per owning team. `gate.teams` in the profile (`name`, `owns` path globs, `approvers`) says who owns which files; the first matching team owns a file, and its approvers plus the leads may certify it, nobody else (a team approver cannot certify another team's files, and still cannot certify a document they gave while another approver exists). New `tools/review_queue.py`: `queue` lists pending files by team, riskiest first; `plan` writes one PR body per team under `_PENDING/_QUEUE/` and names the branch `metis/<date>-review-<team>` (no git, never merges); `certify --team` certifies a team's files across batches. With no `gate.teams` the gate behaves as before.
- Context pack. New `tools/context_pack.py` and skill `metis-context`: for a Jira ticket, graph objects or picked KB files, walks the link graph up and down, collects the hand-written pages that name each object, and writes one pack cut to a token budget. The impact list (every link with its source) comes first and is capped at 40% of the budget; pages are ranked by distance and by whether the object is in the page's first heading; a page is taken whole, else as its headline, else listed under "Not included" with the reason. Every line is copied verbatim, so every `[E: ...]` tag stays. Pages with secrets or card numbers (`sensitive.py`) are left out; stale pages are marked; open questions that block an included page are listed. KB text is wrapped in `<kb-file>` tags with a note that it is data, not instructions. The pack id is a hash of the options and the text, so the same KB and options give the same id. `graph_store.py` gets `upstream` (what an object depends on).
- Coverage report. New `tools/kb_coverage.py`, run by `build_outputs.py`, writes `CROSS-LAYER/COVERAGE.yaml`: graph objects and catalogue tables that no hand-written page names, rejected links with reasons, relations and node types the impact matrix cannot use, open questions by priority and the files they block. It is a name match, not a check that a page is right.
- Impact matrix no longer drops edges silently. `gen_matrix.py` treats `handled_by`, `handled-by` and `handled by` as one relation, lists relations it cannot follow and node types it gives no entry under `not_understood` in `IMPACT_MATRIX.yaml`, and prints a warning.
- Consumption record. New `tools/telemetry.py`: packs and answers note which KB files they used (file names only, no question or answer text) in `<KB>/.metis/usage/`, which is git-ignored; `report` shows what is read and which pages were never used. `metis-ask` records each answer. `kb_manifest.py` skips `.metis/usage/` and `.metis/graph.sqlite`.
- Parser-built links. New `tools/sql_links.py` (optional dependency: `pip install sqlglot`; `build_outputs.py` skips it with a message if sqlglot is missing) reads every .sql file in the source repos and writes `CROSS-LAYER/parsed.links.yaml`: INSERT, CREATE ... AS, MERGE and UPDATE targets read the tables in the statement; dbt models (with `ref`, `source`, `config` and `{% %}` handled) read what they select from; foreign keys give `references`. CTE names are not tables, and a CTE cannot hide a real table of the same name. Each link carries an `[E: file:line "name"]` tag that `verify_kb` resolves; SQL it cannot read is listed under `unparsed`, statements it only reads as a bare command under `partial`. Table-level only, no column lineage.
- Edge origin. `gen_graph.py` puts `origin` on every edge, from the link file's name (`parsed.links.yaml` is `parsed`, `catalog.links.yaml` is `catalogue`, anything else is `ai-proposed`), so a hand-written file cannot claim `parsed`. `graph_store.py` (it rebuilds an old `.sqlite` that lacks the column), the impact matrix and the context pack's impact list show it; the coverage report counts edges by origin and lists the SQL files that were not read.

- New `tools/doc_images.py`: pictures (screenshots, scanned PDFs, picture files, pictures inside Word, PowerPoint, Excel and PDF files) are saved to the scratch folder, the model looks at them, and the text it reads is merged into the document's text at `slide4/image1`-style places so it can be quoted. No OCR program. Picture-read facts are marked in the register and on the published page.
- Lead certification. New `tools/kb_gate.py`: after the first KB is built and locked with a lead's name, nothing enters the KB until a named lead certifies it. Changes are staged under `_PENDING/<batch>/` with a plain-language digest; `certify` and `reject` record who, when and what; leads come from `owners` and `gate.certifiers`; the giver of a document cannot certify it when another lead exists; sources are re-checked after applying and the change is undone on failure; `check` is for CI. State is `.metis/certified.yaml` and `.metis/CERTIFICATIONS.log`.
- New `tools/sensitive.py`: secrets, keys, tokens, SSNs and Luhn-valid card numbers block a page; emails, phones, dates of birth and member or claim ids need a lead to accept them. Used by `doc_kb.py` and `kb_promote.py`. It is pattern-based and cannot find names or free-text health data.
- `doc_kb.py`: with a locked KB, `publish` stages into `_PENDING`; new `approve-restricted`. `doc_intake.py`: Confluence read restrictions and SharePoint scope are captured; unchanged versions are skipped on re-sync; changed ones are noted as a new version.
- Conversation. New always-on rule `metis-conversation`: people talk in plain words and are never told to type a command. All skills are model-triggered only, slash-command wording is gone from skills, rules, templates and messages, and `skills/metis` describes what Mētis can do in plain words. `tools/tests/test_conversation.py` keeps it that way.
- Feedback. New `tools/feedback.py`: after an answer the person can say it was right or wrong. A wrong answer needs why plus either the right answer (raised as a question for a lead, KB unchanged) or a direction to look instead (kept for the asker, shared once a lead approves; `hints` returns it before the next answer). New `tools/golden.py`: competency and golden questions, checked by `static` on every build and by `score` after re-asking. `metis-ask` is rewritten around it, with a one-step `questions.py raise` for "not in the KB".
- Scale and checks.
